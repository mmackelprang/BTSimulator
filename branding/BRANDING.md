# Phantom BLE — brand guide

**Proposed display name:** Phantom BLE
**Tagline:** *Bluetooth devices that aren't there.*

## Why this name

**Phantom** names the trick: peripherals that exist only in software. Alternate: keep *BTSimulator* (exact, flat).

## The mark

A device drawn in dashes — present to the radio, absent from the desk — still broadcasting.
